# Visit Red Sea concierge, version 2: compact chat-only journey

**Current design: Tidal Atelier / التصميم الفوتوغرافي**. The earlier Quiet Tide appearance was replaced following review in the actual chat. Full-photo discovery strips, an editorial Arabic serif, photographic form headers and protected ivory reading surfaces now share one system across all 15 component groups. See [docs/PHOTOGRAPHIC_ATELIER.md](docs/PHOTOGRAPHIC_ATELIER.md). The working booking engine, Quick Replies, recording, verification and PDF export remain in place.

A second, simpler version of `../visitredsea-chat-mockup` (V1, which stays untouched). Same persona, same verified Shebara scenario and prices, same voice recording, real e-mail verification, payment continuation and Red Sea Global branded PDF. What differs:

- **Resort choice happens inside the chat.** Three compact suggestions (Shebara, Desert Rock, Six Senses Southern Dunes, The Red Sea) with a photo, the official name and an اختيار button, plus an استكشف المزيد link to https://www.visitredsea.com/en/resorts (new tab, the conversation stays). The page is never scrolled, dimmed or decorated; no buttons are injected into the page cards. Choosing Shebara continues the journey; the other two get their short details card and the note that the booking can continue with Shebara.
- **Compact components.** Smaller padding, smaller images, only the information needed for the next decision. Villas show image, name, guest capacity, the whole-stay price with its basis (Villa Flat Rate, two nights, taxes included) and the select button; the villa image opens the official resort page (per-villa pages are not evidenced on visitredsea.com, so the verified resort page that lists the accommodation is used). The calendar is smaller on a tinted surface with no white block beneath. Transfer results show only الذهاب / العودة, the product and the price, plus the total. Experiences are three image-and-name options side by side. The booking-summary PDF keeps the full detail.
- **تعديل البيانات in the final review.** Opens the guest form prefilled with the saved values; حفظ التعديلات updates the booking state, refreshes the review and feeds the verification, payment, confirmation and PDF. A changed e-mail resets any verification. Booking selections (dates, villa, airports) are not editable here because only one priced scenario is evidenced.
- **Calmer scrolling.** Sending a message moves to it; the first reply of that turn is brought into view once; later replies and cards never move the reading position. A رسائل جديدة control appears when new content arrived below the viewport and jumps to it only when pressed. Images reserve their space. Closing and reopening keeps the position.
- **Typing status in the header.** المساعد يكتب… replaces "Online" while a reply sequence is still being delivered, so progress is visible even when the in-thread bubble is off screen. It clears when the sequence ends.

**Round 6 (25 October 2026).** Inside the comparison, each Shebara and Desert Rock entry carries a small photo of that resort beside its name (the same image every time, linking to the resort's official page). A Studio carousel card has a single image field, so the thumbnails are an RSG hook (js/chat.js `compareThumbs`, css/studio-rsg.css); other resort cards are unchanged. The recommendation now opens from what the customer actually said (a holiday with his wife) and mentions the sea only as a reason for recommending Shebara.

**Round 5 (25 October 2026).** The Shebara or Desert Rock comparison is now one Studio information card per criterion (setting and access, architecture and stay, views and atmosphere, distinctive experiences, who it suits), each naming what Shebara offers, what Desert Rock offers and the difference; the facts are in `data/resorts.js` (`RESORT_COMPARE`) with their research sources. Returning customers: the approved demo account is an **application-stored profile**, not an RSG customer database record. `private/profiles.seed.json` holds it, and `server.py` inserts it into the profile store (`PROFILE_STORE`, default `private/profiles.json`) once at start; an address already in the store, or seeded before, is never overwritten or re-added. Special requests are never stored or restored: after the code is verified and the profile retrieved, the concierge asks whether the guests have a special request, waits, and only then sends the complete form with the saved details and the request just given (empty if they decline; an unclear answer gets one clarification). Arabic values such as the wife's name are no longer wrapped in an isolating element, so an attached prefix joins them (ولنورة).

**Round 4 (25 October 2026).** Every resort photo in a card (suggestions, details, the comparison and its selection carousel) opens that resort's official page in a new tab; the link is an RSG layer over the Studio card photo, separate from اختيار. A customer undecided between Shebara and Desert Rock gets a comparison (a Studio Carousel of two information cards with the same six facts), then a separate recommendation message, then a two-card selection Carousel. At the guest-details step a Studio Selection List Picker offers «لدي حساب سابق» or «متابعة كضيف جديد». A returning customer types the e-mail in the verification card, receives a real code, and only after `server.py` verifies it does `/api/otp/verify` issue a short-lived session; `/api/profile` returns the saved profile for that session only (never for an e-mail alone). The new form holds only the saved fields, marked with a "saved" icon, never the voice icon. The same verified e-mail is not asked for a second code; a changed e-mail is verified before payment. A new guest (manual or voice) gets no code: review and consent lead straight to the payment hand-off. Saved profiles come from the application's own store named by `PROFILE_STORE` (see `.env.example` and round 5). `server.py` also stops serving dot-files (such as `.env`), `private/` and `.py` files.

**Round 3 (25 October 2026).** Rich components fill the inner chat width (the widget keeps its size). Arabic text in cards and rows keeps an RTL base, and English names, products, airports, amounts, e-mail and URLs are Unicode-isolated inside it (`rtl()` and `bidiPayload()` in `js/chat.js`, applied only to the description, subtitle, instruction and detail-row fields of a Studio payload, within the schema limits). Resort cards carry a one-line description, the setting as the badge, location, accommodation style and a few facilities, from the sourced profiles at the end of `data/resorts.js` (research inventory sections A1 to A11 and 2.11). Quick replies appear once, after the whole agent turn. After a voice message the concierge sends a NEW guest form already filled with the extracted values and the first name, names the required fields still missing, and retires the earlier form. The chat background is a solid colour. The calendar accepts any past, present or future range; ranges other than the quoted stay get the existing "need to confirm the price" reply instead of a quote. Both transfer legs show a photo.

**Empathy Studio rich components (22 October 2026).** The cards, pickers and reply buttons in the chat are now rendered by the Empathy Studio web-SDK renderers: `studio/studio-renderers.js` is a line-for-line port of the SDK's `rich-components/*` sources, `studio/studio-components.css` is the SDK's own CSS, `studio/studio-rtl.css` mirrors its physical left/right rules inside the RTL host, `studio/schemas.js` holds the registered Studio schemas (every payload is checked against them at render time; a mismatch is logged, never shown) and `studio/icons/` carries the Lucide icons the SDK fetches by name. Mapping: the three resort suggestions are a Carousel with اختيار buttons (استكشف المزيد stays an RSG link under it), resort details and transfer legs and experiences are Carousel information cards, villas are a Carousel with اختيار buttons, the stay summary, the updated totals, the final review (with تعديل البيانات as its secondary button and the terms acknowledgement as an RSG control under the card), the payment hand-off and the final confirmation are Confirmation Cards whose primary button carries the line the stage already accepts, arrival modes are a Selection List Picker, airports are the Sectioned Multi-Select (grouped list picker, one chip per group, the optional flight fields were dropped) and quick replies are the Studio `buttons` component. A Studio button sends its payload as the customer message; `js/chat.js` maps it to the same `act()` transitions as before. The calendar, the guest form with the voice composer and the e-mail verification keep their RSG components (no Studio equivalent). Elements with no slot in a Studio component sit in a small RSG aside under the card: the official page links, the consent wording, the PDF status. The payment button opens the exact checkout URL and starts the five-second continuation; تحميل PDF builds the same PDF without echoing a customer line. `css/studio-rsg.css` holds only the RSG hooks (theme variables, placement, retired and busy states, the aside).

## Run it

```bash
start.cmd
```

V2 defaults to port 8090 (or the next free one), so it can run beside V1 on 8080. Equivalent: `python server.py 8090`. Mail settings come from `.env` in this folder (copied from V1; keep it private) or from environment variables, exactly as in V1. `index.html?walkthrough=1` renders the whole V2 route as a document; it is the source of `RSG_Full_Conversation_V2.pdf`.

Everything else (data files, engine, server, OTP rules, evidence rules, licensing) is as documented in the V1 README and `docs/`.

`tools/responsive-preview.html` embeds the real application and switches between desktop, 390 px and 360 px without restarting its conversation. Add `?recorded=1` to inspect the existing recorded walkthrough inside the actual chat shell. That explicit visual-review mode makes no email, microphone or checkout requests and does not establish real verification or payment results. The normal application keeps server-backed verification.

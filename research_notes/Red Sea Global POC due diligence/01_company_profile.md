# Red Sea Global (RSG): company profile notes for AI booking-agent POC due diligence

Research date and access date for every source: 12 October 2026.

How to read these notes

- Labels: [OFFICIAL-DOC] = RSG, PIF or Vision 2030 owned page that was opened and read. [THIRD-PARTY] = other publisher, opened and read. [CONFLICT] = two opened sources disagree, both shown. "Not verified" = no opened source supports it, with the evidence needed.
- Status words are kept apart on purpose: announced, planned, under development, open (per a press release), bookable (not tested in this workstream).
- Citations use source IDs ([S01] etc.). Every ID resolves to a URL in the source register and in the link definitions at the end of the file.
- Retrieval method: redseaglobal.com and pif.gov.sa returned HTTP 403 to the WebFetch tool, so pages were retrieved by direct HTTP GET with a browser user agent and converted to text. Text hidden behind scripts, "Show more +" toggles, images, video and PDF or Issuu viewers was not captured.
- A marketing page or press release establishes what RSG says. Nothing here was verified on the ground or through a booking engine.

## 1. Official name and publicly named legal entities

### Takeaway
The public name is "Red Sea Global" (RSG), in Arabic "البحر الأحمر الدولية" (the Arabic terms page writes "شركة البحر الأحمر الدولية"). RSG describes itself as a closed joint stock company owned by PIF, but no commercial registration number appears on the pages read, and the site gives three different registered or office addresses.

### Takeaway
RSG says it is a closed joint stock company wholly owned by the Public Investment Fund and calls itself "a PIF company". PIF lists RSG under its giga-projects. The board is chaired by the Crown Prince, who also chairs PIF.

### Cited Findings

- [OFFICIAL-DOC] Privacy Policy: "a closed joint stock company owned by the Public Investment Fund of Saudi Arabia". Source: [S04]

- [OFFICIAL-DOC] PIF website places RSG at Our Investments > Giga-Projects > Red Sea Global, with "Launched 2018" and "Leading the development of luxury destinations along Saudi Arabia's west coast". The page meta description calls it "PIF’s Giga-project Red Sea Global". Source: [S30]

- Ownership wording is consistent across RSG and PIF sources. Individual resorts can have different owners (joint ventures) and different operators, so "an RSG resort" does not imply RSG is the sole owner or the operator.

### Inferences
- Inventory is changing monthly, so any agent knowledge base built from RSG marketing pages will be out of date and internally inconsistent unless it reads from a maintained source of truth (for example Visit Red Sea's live catalogue) and stores an "as of" date per fact.
- Because RSG headlines use "opening" before guests can stay, the agent needs an explicit status per property: announced, opening announced, accepting reservations, welcoming guests.
- The absence of any announced guest-facing AI or app channel suggests the POC would not duplicate a publicly launched product, but internal or unannounced tools cannot be ruled out from public sources.

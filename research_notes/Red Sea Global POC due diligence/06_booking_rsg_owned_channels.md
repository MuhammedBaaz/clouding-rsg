# Booking journeys on RSG-owned and RSG-branded channels (visitredsea.com, amaala.com, Red Sea Global Hospitality engines)

Research date: 12 October 2026. All interactive observations were made between 18:45 and 19:19 UTC (21:45 to 22:19 KSA, the engine footer clock showed "09:48 PM" at 18:48 UTC). Method: built-in browser, own tab, cookie banner declined on visitredsea.com (OneTrust "Cookies Settings" then "Confirm My Choices" with all optional categories off). No name, email, phone, card or other identity data was typed anywhere. No account was created, no sign-in attempted, no terms box ticked, no submit, confirm, pay or enquiry button pressed.

Evidence labels used below:
- [OBSERVED-LIVE] rendered page text or control seen in the browser.
- [OBSERVED-LIVE, network] request or response of the page's own same-origin booking API, read in the browser. Where marked "probe", I re-issued the identical search payload the UI sends (dates and guest counts only) from the page context to test more date sets quickly. Probe results are API output, not rendered screens.
- [OFFICIAL-DOC] static policy or FAQ text on an RSG site.
- [CONFLICT] two RSG sources disagree.
- "Not verified" means the step or state was not reached.

Scenario key: A = 2 adults, 1 room, 12 to 15 Jan 2027. B = 2 adults + 2 children (5 and 10), 1 room, 14 to 18 Mar 2027. C = 4 adults, 2 rooms, 12 to 15 Jan 2027. A-alt = scenario A guests on 10 to 13 Nov 2026. B-alt = scenario B guests on 15 to 19 Nov 2026. C-alt = scenario C guests on 10 to 13 Nov 2026.

Source IDs (S1 to S33) are defined in the source register in the last section. Every finding cites one.

## 1. Does visitredsea.com or amaala.com have its own search or booking widget, and what does submitting it do?

### Takeaway
Yes, both. Each site has a header BOOK widget with three tabs (Resorts, Experiences, Transportation) that keeps the guest on the same domain and runs a 5-step booking flow under `/en/booking/...`, backed by a same-origin API at `/api/booking/v1/...`. It sells RSG-operated and third-party-operated resorts alike, with no handoff to operator engines.

### Gaps
- The amaala.com widget was opened and its API read, but no UI search was submitted on amaala.com. Its results pages are assumed, not observed.
- "ADD ANOTHER RESORT" (multi-resort itinerary) was not exercised.
- A standalone Experiences search was not submitted. Only the widget fields and the in-flow experiences step were observed.

## 2. For each RSG-owned property, what is the route from resort page to booking engine, and what survives each domain change?

### Takeaway
Each RSG-operated resort has two live booking routes that do not share a session. Route 1 is the on-domain visitredsea.com (or amaala.com) engine. Route 2 starts on the brand site (shebara.sa, desertrock.sa, turtlebayhotel.sa), passes through `reservations.redseaglobalhospitality.com`, and lands on a per-brand subdomain (`reservations.shebara.sa`, `reservations.desertrock.sa`, `reservations.turtlebayhotel.sa`). On Route 2 every selection is carried in the URL, so it survives the domain change.

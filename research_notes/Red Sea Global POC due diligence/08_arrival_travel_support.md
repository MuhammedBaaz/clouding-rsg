# Arrival, travel requirements, transfers and guest support for The Red Sea and AMAALA (as of 12 October 2026)

Research date and access date for every source: 12 October 2026. Nothing here comes from prior knowledge. Every claim cites a page that was opened on that date. Search snippets were used as leads only and are marked where a lead could not be opened.

How to read the labels:

- [GOV-OFFICIAL] Saudi government body (Saudi Tourism Authority / Visit Saudi, Ministry of Tourism eVisa portal, ZATCA, SFDA).
- [RSG-OFFICIAL] Red Sea Global owned sites (visitredsea.com, amaala.com, rsiairport.sa, redseaglobal.com).
- [OPERATOR-OFFICIAL] the operator of the specific service (Fly Red Sea, Red Sea Global Hospitality for Shebara, airlines).
- [THIRD-PARTY] anything else.
- [CONFLICT] two opened sources disagree. Both are shown.
- "Not verified" means no opened source states it. The evidence needed is named.

Three categories are kept apart throughout: (A) Saudi government travel requirements, (B) destination, resort or operator policies, (C) information the booking flow requests. Category C is based on UI text strings embedded in the visitredsea.com page source. The live booking form was not walked, because that would mean entering personal data.

Source IDs (S01 etc.) resolve in the source register at the end. Time-sensitive items are flagged "TIME-SENSITIVE".

## 1. What official arrival guidance do visitredsea.com and amaala.com publish?

### Takeaway
Both sites carry a three-page "Plan your trip" section (Airport & Flights, Weather & Proximity, Getting to & Around) plus a per-resort "getting here" block. The Red Sea is presented as served by Red Sea International Airport (RSI). AMAALA is presented as served primarily by Al Wajh International Airport (EJH), with RSI as a second gateway. The guidance is thin on prices, schedules and booking mechanics, and it contradicts itself on which routes exist.

### Inferences
- The official arrival copy is marketing-level. An assistant cannot answer "how much is the transfer" or "what time is the last boat" from these pages. Those answers have to come from the booking engine or customer care.
- The route statements on the two destination sites are not maintained together (see section 2 conflicts), so the assistant should treat the flight widget as the freshest RSG signal and still tell guests to confirm with the airline.
- The Yanbu and AlUla entries in the Arabic data, together with the booking-flow string "transportation isn't available from your selected airport" (section 3), suggest the booking flow supports arrival airports other than RSI. This is an inference from page data, not a published statement.

## 2. Which airports serve each destination, and what do official sources say about airlines, routes and facilities?

### Takeaway
RSI serves The Red Sea and is the secondary gateway for AMAALA. Al Wajh (EJH) reopened in mid-2026 and is the primary AMAALA gateway, with Saudia domestic flights only. As of 12 October 2026 the Visit Red Sea flight widget shows Saudia (Riyadh, Jeddah), flydubai (Dubai), Qatar Airways (Doha) and Etihad (Abu Dhabi) at RSI. The RSI airport site itself publishes no route list or schedule, and its partners page omits Etihad.

## 3. How do guests get from airport to resort?

### Takeaway
Transfers are pre-booked through Visit Red Sea customer care or the booking flow, not bought on arrival. RSI publishes hard rules: all transfers booked at least 48 hours ahead, Fly Red Sea seaplanes at least seven days ahead, a per-guest luggage limit, and no seaplane booking on the day. Mode depends on the resort: car only for inland and Shura Island resorts, car plus boat or seaplane for Ummahat and Sheybarah islands. No source publishes seaplane operating hours, last boat times or passenger weight limits.

### Cited Findings

- [RSG-OFFICIAL] "All transfers must be pre-booked at least 48 hours before your arrival to ensure a smooth journey." Contact for booking: "customer.care@visitredsea.com or call +966 011 2222 222". [S21](https://rsiairport.sa/en/arrival/from-airport-transfers/)
- [RSG-OFFICIAL] "all arriving guests are expected to have a pre-booked transfer before arriving to The Red Sea." [S28](https://rsiairport.sa/en/arrival/welcome-center/)
- [RSG-OFFICIAL] Without a booking: "you will have to bypass the Welcome Centre and go directly to the arrival pavilion for assistance in making a new booking – please note that booking a seaplane transfer with Fly Red Sea will not be possible at that time." ### Inferences
- The assistant must ask for arrival airport, airline, flight number and arrival time before it can offer a transfer, and must ask lead-time questions first: under 7 days rules out a seaplane, under 48 hours breaches the published general rule, under 24 hours goes to live chat.
- For Ummahat and Sheybarah resorts the assistant needs a mode choice (seaplane, shared speedboat, private speedboat, yacht upgrade) and a luggage count, because excess bags travel separately and cost extra.
- Self-drivers need different handling by resort: island and Shura guests park at Turtle Bay and need a ride reservation (Shura also a gate pass), and the booking flow collects driver and vehicle data for a Car Entry Permit.
- Whether a transfer is free or charged is not a single rule. A complimentary shuttle is published for five named stops. Private cars, boats, yachts and seaplanes appear as priced lines in the booking flow.

### Gaps
- Not verified: seaplane operating hours or a daylight-only rule. No opened source states one. Requires Fly Red Sea terms of carriage or customer care confirmation.
- Not verified: boat or shuttle timetables, first and last departures. Requires customer care.
- Not verified: passenger weight limits or body-weight declaration for seaplanes. Only baggage limits are published.
- Not verified: transfer prices (private car, shared boat, private boat, yacht, seaplane transfer seat). Requires a booking-flow quote.
- Not verified: the full Transportation Services cancellation policy text. The link exists in the flow but the document was not reachable without starting a booking.
- Not verified: which booking-flow fields are mandatory, and at which step passport, visa and nationality are requested. Requires a walked booking.
- Not verified: Fly Red Sea service to AMAALA in operation. amaala.com says transfers "are available"; flyredsea.com describes RSI to Ummahat and Sheybarah only; RSG's 2024 release calls AMAALA a future connection. Requires Fly Red Sea confirmation.

## 4. What do official Saudi government sources say about tourist entry?

### Takeaway
There is no universal rule. Visit Saudi (Saudi Tourism Authority) sorts 247 countries and territories into eight groups, and for the largest group the answer changes again with the traveller's residency or existing US, UK or Schengen visa. Fees, insurance inclusion, the portal used and the rules for minors all differ by group. No government or RSG source publishes a special visa arrangement for The Red Sea or AMAALA.

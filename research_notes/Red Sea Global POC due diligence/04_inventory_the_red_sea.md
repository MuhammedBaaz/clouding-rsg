# Property inventory: The Red Sea destination (Red Sea Global)

Research date and access date for every source: 12 October 2026. Scope: The Red Sea destination only (Shura Island, Ummahat islands, Sheybarah Island, inland and coastal resorts). AMAALA and Thuwal are out of scope and are only mentioned where a lead had to be rejected.

Labels used: [OFFICIAL-DOC] published statement on an RSG or operator page or press release that I opened. [OBSERVED-LIVE] behaviour or data I observed on a live page (widget contents, button targets, embedded page data). [THIRD-PARTY] non-official source. [CONFLICT] two opened sources disagree. "Not verified (requires ...)" where I could not open evidence. Source IDs (S01 to S65) resolve in the source register near the end.

## Takeaway

- visitredsea.com lists 13 resorts under The Red Sea. 11 of them are selectable in the site's booking widget and are the same 11 that RSG press releases count as open. Grand Hyatt The Red Sea and Raffles The Red Sea have full detail pages but are not selectable in the widget, and RSG's own Shura Island page labels both "Coming Soon". [OBSERVED-LIVE S01, S02] [OFFICIAL-DOC S25, S38, S39]
- RSG's Shura Island page labels four more resorts "Coming Soon" that are not on visitredsea.com at all: Fairmont The Red Sea, Rosewood Red Sea, Jumeirah The Red Sea, Faena The Red Sea. That gives 17 named properties. RSG states "18 hotels in total" for the destination, so one hotel is unnamed in everything I opened. [OFFICIAL-DOC S24, S25]
- Thuwal Private Retreat is not part of The Red Sea destination. RSG presents it as a separate, third destination near Jeddah. [OFFICIAL-DOC S24, S30, S35]
- The visitredsea.com resort pages and the operators' own pages disagree on several policies (cancellation windows at Shebara, Desert Rock, Turtle Bay, Miraval). An agent that quotes policy must name which channel the policy belongs to. See the conflict list.

## Limitations

- hyatt.com blocked every access method. I did not attempt to defeat its bot protection.
- redseaglobal.com returned intermittent 403s to curl; all RSG pages cited were eventually retrieved with HTTP 200.
- Two sources (S53 text, S55) were read through a summarising fetch tool, so quoted wording from them carries a small risk of paraphrase. S53 links and contacts were confirmed in the browser.
- visitredsea.com pages carry no visible "last updated" date. Policy text there may lag the operator's own site, as conflicts 1 to 5 suggest.
- No availability search, form submission or account creation was performed.

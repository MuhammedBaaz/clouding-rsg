## 3. Problems that affect the edit

1. **Phone number mismatch.** The guest enters and reviews `+966 5029784` in the chat (07:07.5 to 07:40). The booking PDF opened at 08:15.5 prints `+966 5047892`. The ERP panel and Faisal's WhatsApp show a third, fictional number, `+966 55 123 4567`. The PDF most likely came from a booking record saved in an earlier session for the same quote, which would mean the PDF does not reflect this session's details. That is a product issue worth a separate check. For the film it has to be blurred or re-recorded.
2. **Personal data on screen.** `muhammed.baz@clouding.ai` appears in the details form, the Chrome autofill dropdown (07:00), the review card and the PDF. The handoff transcript uses the fictional `mohammed.baz@example.com` for the same guest.
3. **Browser chrome in every recording.** The tab strip, the address bar (`127.0.0.1:8100`, `trip.html?b=RSGD-6CBAC3F541&t=…`, `C:/Users/Compumarts/Downloads/VisitRedSea-trip-2026-10-23 (10).pdf`) and a bookmarks bar with personal entries (Inbox, Salesforce, ChatGPT and others) sit in the top 150 px or so of every frame. Crop to the page area throughout. The "(10)" in the PDF filename also shows there were earlier takes.
4. **Older chat build at the start of recordings 2 and 3.** Before each overlay opens (ERP 00:00.0 to 00:01.4, Concierge 00:00.0 to 00:01.2), the widget shows `PDF تحميل` as a guest message and `ملف تفاصيل رحلتك جاهز يا محمد: تحميل ملف PDF` from the agent. Recording 1 has neither (the final build opens the PDF without a chat turn). Start both clips after the overlay opens.
5. **The guest's voice note is weak.** The only guest voice note that is played back (02:30.8 to 02:35.0) has about 1.9 s of sound (02:32.1 to 02:34.0). Whisper returned low-confidence gibberish. In context it could be «إيه… بس أنا وزوجتي» ("yes, just me and my wife"), but that is a guess, not a transcript. The agent's three voice replies are clean.
6. **Duplicated summary.** After a second, unplayed guest voice note (03:13.5), the agent re-posts the same stay summary and card (03:28.5) with a voice version (03:37). Nothing new happens between 03:02.5 and 03:45.0.
7. **No airport chip is ever tapped.** The airport choice was spoken in a voice note that is not played back (05:07.5). The transfer cards show the outcome (Yanbu in, RSIA out), but the choice itself is not visible.
8. **Raw markdown while the payment link streams.** From 07:45.5 to 07:49.0 the link shows as `[المتابعة للدفع](https://www.visitredsea.com/en/booking/checkout?...` before it renders as an underlined link by 07:54.0. Use the rendered state only.
9. **Loyalty line.** The trip card shows 3,379 points earned next to «الرصيد المتاح 0 نقطة» (available balance 0 points). A viewer could read that as a contradiction. Don't hold on that line.
10. **The kayak outcome uses a booking verb.** Faisal writes «حجزت لكم الكاياك يوم السبت 24 أكتوبر، وبأكد لك الوقت بالضبط قبل الموعد». It is a scripted concierge message, and the transcript notes that kayak availability is not verified. The narration should describe it as Faisal arranging the kayak and promising to confirm the time, never as a confirmed reservation.
11. **Dead air.** About 3:17 of the agent recording's 8:40 is waiting, false starts or duplicates. The details are in the log below. Recordings 2 and 3 are silent throughout (-91 dB digital silence) and need music and narration.

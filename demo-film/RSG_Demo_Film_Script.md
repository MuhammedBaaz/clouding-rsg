Narration for the 04:49 demo film · English · Draft v1 · 12 November 2026 · Clouding AI

**Journey:** a guest's first «السلام عليكم» on the Visit Red Sea page → a name Rafiq keeps using → a resort chosen on the page itself → villa, party, dates and the stay total → transfers and the updated total → an editable review and the guest's booking PDF → the resort's booking system assigns a personal concierge and sends him the internal guest file → the concierge introduces himself the same afternoon and, two days later, arranges a kayak outing and promises to confirm the time.

The film carries no English subtitles: the Arabic on screen is left as it is, and the narration gives the meaning in English.

**Runtime and read time:** the film runs 04:49.2 (289.2 s). The narration is 483 words, about 214 s of read time at 140 words per minute including pauses. The rest is deliberate: the voice notes, replies landing on screen, the held totals, the chapter cards and the silent end. Don't slide a line earlier to fill a gap.
